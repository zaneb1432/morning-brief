## 1. TECH & AI NEWS

**Anthropic launches Claude Duet, a proactive "chief of staff" agent.** On October 21 Anthropic announced Claude Duet as a research preview. It's an always-on agent that sends a short morning brief across your connected apps, checks in every few hours, and messages you only when something needs you. You can hand it work by text or voice, from scheduling to building dashboards or sales forecasts. It asks for approval before sending messages, making payments, signing documents or deleting files. It runs on Claude Opus 5.5 on the web and in a standalone iOS app, and counts against your existing plan limits. Access is by waitlist for US Pro and Max subscribers. Anthropic admits it "can still misjudge what's urgent." Why it matters: the product category is shifting from chatbots you query to agents that watch your work and act first. Coverage frames Duet as Anthropic's answer to Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots. Sources: [MacRumors](https://macrumors.com/2026/10/21/anthropic-claude-duet/), [Thurrott](https://thurrott.com/a-i/anthropic/343121/anthropic-announces-claude-duet-a-personal-ai-agent).

**AI-assisted discovery of two critical PHP flaws sends governments scrambling.** On October 21, two vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, CVSS 9.2) were disclosed in PHP. Either lets an unauthenticated attacker take over a web server with a single crafted request. mod_php setups are most exposed, because the attack can fire before any application code runs. Wiz says it found the bugs, hidden for over a decade, using its own AI research tool plus models from Google and OpenAI. Several government sites went offline as a precaution, and the NSA advised patching immediately or isolating internet-facing apps. Exposure estimates vary, from "up to 70% of websites" to Censys data suggesting about 1.5 million of 2.6 million hosts run unpatched versions. Why it matters: it is a concrete case of AI finding decade-old bugs at scale. Defenders got there first this time, but the patch window is the new race. Sources: [ABC7](https://abc7news.com/story/urgent-security-fix-issued-code-vital-estimated-70-websites/19965216/), [Deseret News](https://www.deseret.com/business/2026/10/21/website-computer-server-security-flaw-artificial-intelligence-hackers-cybersecurity-php-programming-language/), [Canberra Times](https://www.canberratimes.com.au/story/9373060/government-websites-go-dark-after-ai-discovers-flaw/).

**New Mexico judge orders Meta to pay $25 billion over deceptive privacy and moderation practices.** On October 21 Judge Francis Mathew imposed the penalty after a jury found roughly 44 million violations of the state's Unfair Practices Act. At $5,000 per violation, the statutory maximum, he wrote the state was entitled to closer to $220 billion but chose a lower figure. New Mexico had asked for $35–40 billion, and Meta argued for no more than about $3.4 billion (one outlet gives a different cap). Meta will appeal, calling the award "shocking to the conscience." Interest accrues at 15% a year. New Mexico went to trial alone, while 48 other states took an $18 billion settlement in August. Why it matters: it is a very large consumer-protection penalty against a big tech platform, and the appeal on due-process grounds will test how far state statutes can scale. Sources: [Source NM](https://sourcenm.com/2026/10/21/new-mexico-judge-orders-meta-to-pay-state-25b/), [ABC30](https://abc30.com/story/judge-orders-meta-pay-25-billion-violating-new-mexico-law-cambridge-analytica-data-breach/19965355/).

**Musk says Tesla and SpaceX have formed committees to explore a merger.** On SpaceX's October 20 earnings webcast, Musk said independent-director committees are studying a combination, and that he is recusing himself because of his conflicts. No terms are agreed, a deal "may not" happen, and any deal would need a majority of all outstanding Tesla shares, which he called a high bar. He said "there's a lot to be gained from cooperating on developing superintelligence software." Reported combined value is about $3–3.5 trillion, and Barclays has floated an announcement before Tesla's December 3 shareholder meeting. Why it matters: it would be the largest merger ever, and it ties the AI-compute buildout to Musk's other companies. Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/economy/2026/10/21/elon-musk-says-spacex-and-tesla-exploring-largest-merger-in), [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/musk-says-spacex-tesla-have-formed-committees-to-explore-merger).

## 4. FOUNDER BIOGRAPHY — Madam C.J. Walker (1867–1919)

*Written from general historical knowledge, not this week's searches. First edition, so no repeats yet.*

Sarah Breedlove was born December 23, 1867 in Delta, Louisiana, the first child in her family born free after emancipation. Orphaned at seven, she was married at fourteen, had a daughter, Lelia, in 1885, and was widowed by about 1887. She moved to St. Louis and worked as a washerwoman for roughly a dollar or two a day, determined to send Lelia to school.

In the 1890s she began losing her hair to scalp disease, a common problem when homes lacked running water and people washed rarely. She worked as a sales agent for Annie Turnbo Malone, whose Poro company sold Black haircare products, then moved to Denver and in 1905 married newspaperman Charles J. Walker. She became "Madam C.J. Walker" and launched her own product line in 1906, the "Wonderful Hair Grower."

Her edge was distribution. She sold door to door and by mail, and trained a network of commissioned "beauty culturists" who earned independence at a time when few jobs were open to Black women. She set up a college in Pittsburgh in 1908 and moved her headquarters and factory to Indianapolis in 1910. By the mid-1910s the Madam C.J. Walker Manufacturing Company had thousands of agents. In 1917 it held a national convention in Philadelphia, where she praised agents who had built their own businesses.

Her wealth funded activism. She gave to the NAACP's anti-lynching campaign, the Indianapolis YMCA and Black colleges, and in 1918 built Villa Lewaro, a mansion in Irvington, New York. She died May 25, 1919, at 51. She is often called America's first self-made female millionaire, though historians note that Malone built a comparable business earlier. The lesson for founders is that she built a system, product plus trained sales force plus brand, rather than a single product.
